Our story

A true 1950s classic

A vintage motor lodge that has truly stood the test of time, and still treats every guest like a neighbor.

Since the 1950s

Warm hospitality, retro style

Mid-Town Motel is a vintage motor lodge with 1950s origins and decades of friendly service. We are a two-minute walk from the Boothbay Harbor waterfront, shops and restaurants, in a quiet, landscaped setting.

Our goal is simple: warm, friendly service and real comfort, so Mid-Town is the perfect home base for exploring midcoast Maine.
