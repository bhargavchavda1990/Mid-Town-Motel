Welcome

Vintage charm, modern comfort

Mid-Town Motel is a vintage motor lodge with 1950s roots and decades of warm hospitality. We are a two-minute walk from Boothbay Harbor's waterfront, shops, galleries and restaurants, tucked into a quiet, landscaped setting.

Park the car, stroll to dinner, and come home to a comfy bed. It really is that easy.
