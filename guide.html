Things to do

Boothbay Harbor guide

Seaside scenery, fresh seafood, schooner sails, beaches and villages: the best of the area, picked for you.

Around Boothbay

Beaches

Beaches

Grimes Cove

A small rocky beach at the end of Route 96 with views of nearby islands. Popular with photographers and artists.

Beaches

Pemaquid Beach Park

A sandy beach with a snack bar, restrooms, a beach house for changing and rinsing off, and a picnic area.

Beaches

Reid State Park

Two miles of hiking trails, a long sandy beach, shaded picnic tables with ocean views and public restrooms.

Beaches

Popham Beach State Park

Walking, ocean swimming and reading, with ample parking, clean facilities and a picnic area.

Around Boothbay

On the water

On the water

Cabbage Island Clambakes

Sail the Bennie Alice from Pier 6 to Cabbage Island for a traditional Downeast clambake cooked at the water's edge. Seven days a week from late June through the weekend after Labor Day.

22 Commercial Street · (207) 633-7200
Visit website →
On the water

Eastwind Schooner

A two-hour sail aboard a Boothbay Harbor schooner. Bring your own food and drinks, plus a windbreaker.

20 Commercial Street · (207) 633-6598
Visit website →
On the water

Monhegan Island

A day trip about 10 miles offshore: hike some of the highest cliffs on the Maine coast, visit a brewery and galleries, and see a shipwreck. Ferries leave from Port Clyde.

Visit website →
On the water

Lighthouse tour

A self-guided lighthouse tour for boaters, covering the region's five lighthouses.

Gardens & art

Coastal Maine Botanical Gardens

Founded in 1996 and spread over 270 acres of tidal shoreland, with flower-lined paths and seasonal art exhibits.

105 Botanical Gardens Drive · (207) 633-8000

Gardens & art

Boothbay Region Land Trust

Nature preserves including Porter Preserve and Ovens Mouth Preserve. Pack a picnic from Trevett Country Store.

60 Samoset Rd · (207) 633-4818

Gardens & art

Sculpture Boothbay

Maine's only public sculpture trail, from downtown Boothbay Harbor to East Boothbay and Southport Island. A map at the front desk shows each piece.

191 Shore Road · (207) 633-2353
